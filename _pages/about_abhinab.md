Abhinab is a PhD Candidate in the Computer Science department at VT.
He works with Dr. Adrian Sandu in the Computational Science Lab ([CSL](https://csl.cs.vt.edu/)). 
His broad research interests span inverse problems, data assimilation, bayesian inference, optimization in machine learning.
His current project includes creating data assimilation techniques applicable to digital twins and parameter estimation using several bayesian inversion.
