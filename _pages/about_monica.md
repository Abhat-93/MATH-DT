Monica Shanmugam is a Ph.D. student in the [CREATe](https://create.centers.vt.edu) Lab at Virginia Tech. She is currently contributing to the NSF-funded MATH-DT project, bringing her background in experimental testing and CFD modeling to support the development of digital twin frameworks for jet engines. Her research interests include intelligent propulsion systems and data-driven modeling for engine diagnostics and health monitoring. She is passionate about advancing predictive capabilities in engine health monitoring and performance diagnostics through innovative research and interdisciplinary collaboration. Outside of research, Monica is passionate about supporting underrepresented groups in STEM. She serves as Media Head for Women of Aeronautics and Astronautics (WoAA) India, where she helps highlight the contributions of women and other minority voices in aerospace.
